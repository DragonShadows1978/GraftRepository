## Part B — Quick surface scan

Static findings only; none was exercised or fixed.

1. **Final-forward demand abort can become an exception.** [grm_demand.py:401–404](../core/grm_demand.py#L401) sets aborted_at even on the final cache-commit forward. [graft_arena.py:4396–4411](../core/graft_arena.py#L4396) swallows that abort expecting the unused row to be dropped, but [grm_demand.py:495–499](../core/grm_demand.py#L495) drops it only when aborted_at is absent. A first fire exclusively on that flush leaves ngen+1 rows and raises DemandError. This contradicts the config's unconditional claim that early abort cannot change serving.

2. **Receipt projection loses RT1 provenance.** [grm_admission.py:288–305](../core/grm_admission.py#L288) emits four demotion/family/ranking fields. [grm_three_pass.py:450–465](../core/grm_three_pass.py#L450) does not pass the admission prefix, and its [fixed admission projection:660–690](../core/grm_three_pass.py#L660) omits those fields. Raw info may retain them; the canonical route receipt does not.

3. **Capture provenance is not durable on the ordinary manifest path.** [graft_arena.py:852–865](../core/graft_arena.py#L852) attaches capture metadata at graft top level. [graft_repository.py:4619–4635](../core/graft_repository.py#L4619) omits it when serializing; [reload:4769–4784](../core/graft_repository.py#L4769) does not restore it. Payload persistence is separate; the finding is lost evidence about how that payload was captured.

4. **“Grounded” is weaker than relationally correct.** [graft_arena.py:2725–2743](../core/graft_arena.py#L2725) accepts one overlapping substantive word for prose, or identifier coverage pooled across mounts. Neither checks entity–value association or negation. [grm_text_norm.py:45–57](../core/grm_text_norm.py#L45) correctly warns that global separator folding would weaken this further.

5. **WC1's seat column counts grafts.** [grm_wc1_results.py:112–114](../scripts/grm_wc1_results.py#L112) and [161–182](../scripts/grm_wc1_results.py#L161) use the length of mount_fitted, not summed token seats. Thus 0.9 “resident seats” is not a residency measurement. Total wall time also explicitly includes lock contention. The recall cells survive these reporting defects.

## Part D — The crazy ideas

These are experiments, not novelty claims. Ranking uses **upside U (1–5) × subjective probability of passing the stated first gate**, not probability of becoming a product. **★ marks the three I would run first:** they cheaply challenge addressability, grounding and the detector's causal interpretation. P-tags point to the prior-art register in Part E.

Cross-track constraints: [SP3](/mnt/Shared/APA_SP3_Model_Test_Report_2026-09-07.md) supports a better precision selector on MiniCPM3; [SP4G](/mnt/Shared/APA_SP4G_Gemma4_Model_Test_Report_2026-09-08.md) separates per-call exactness from a failed strict model-floor comparison. [SP5](/mnt/Shared/APA_SP5_GPTOSS20B_Model_Test_Report_2026-09-08.md) has model/operator comparisons and a streamed 16K attention run, not GRM recall certification. None establishes that APA speeds this small-arena workload; raw GPT-OSS perplexity must not become a memory-capability gate.

### 1. ★ Give memories virtual addresses — U5 × 75% = 3.75

Route identifier questions to a stable entity–relation address, then resolve its current version to native graft pages instead of letting duplicate children compete in a global ranking. Treat a requested address absent from the mounted set as a page fault before generation, leaving topical discovery on the existing router.

**If it works:** point recall is insensitive to split-family multiplicity and many demand decisions need no attention telemetry. **Falsifier:** 24 held-out alias/correction queries, 1–100 duplicated decoys, exact-oracle metadata first; ≤0.5 GPU-h. **Kill:** ranking dependence persists even with correct addresses, or natural alias resolution erases the benefit. **Spec:** preserved. **Art:** P1/P2; new proposal is this GRM comparison.

### 2. ★ Make memory carry relational witnesses — U5 × 60% = 3.00

Store source-bound entity–relation–value spans as admission and verification metadata alongside native K/V. Require each asserted binding to have a witness from one source version, and explicitly represent joins when an answer needs two sources.

**If it works:** high lexical overlap cannot certify a wrong relation. **Falsifier:** CPU-check 40 role-swapped, negated and scattered-token answers, then ≤0.5 GPU-h on 24 live questions with witnesses hidden from the model prompt. **Kill:** extraction misses ordinary paraphrases or rejection destroys correct answers. **Spec:** preserves K/V recall; strengthens the existing grounding guard. **Art:** P2/P5; no claim that metadata replaces contextualization.

### 3. Query-shaped pages, fixed geometry — U4 × 70% = 2.80

Deposit several overlapping native graft views of a fact, such as a compact binding and its surrounding explanation, while keeping the serving arena's positional geometry fixed. Select the smallest sufficient view and shuttle to context only when the question requires it.

**If it works:** WC1's chunk benefit survives at width 96 without surrendering relation context. **Falsifier:** same 24 documents, flat versus two-scale views, unseen single-fact and two-fact queries; ≤0.75 GPU-h. **Kill:** views drop bindings, amplify routing competitors, or cost more deposit/storage than they save. **Spec:** preserved. **Art:** P1/P2/P4; this extends house P2C/WC1 rather than inventing chunking.

### 4. Experts know verbs; GRM supplies nouns — U4 × 60% = 2.40

Train a small expert on procedures while every entity, quantity and exception remains a freshly deposited graft. Evaluate whether the combination transfers a procedure to entirely unseen variable bindings that neither component handles alone.

**If it works:** the MoE and GRM tracks compose without asking weights to memorize arbitrary episodes. **Falsifier:** a frozen 2×2 expert/graft ablation over 32 procedural problems with disjoint entity/value sets; ≤1 GPU-h using a small existing model. **Kill:** gain survives without grafts through leakage, disappears on new bindings, or harms generic controls. **Spec:** frozen base preserved; optional adapter explicitly separate. **Art:** P4/P6.

### 5. ★ Lesion the memory to measure whether it mattered — U4 × 55% = 2.20

Fork a captured live attempt and remove or exchange one mount while holding all other state constant. Use the change in a one-token answer distribution as a causal diagnostic beside attention mass, rather than assuming that attention paid to a memory means the memory supplied the fact.

**If it works:** wrong confident reads can be distinguished from unused correct memories. **Falsifier:** 20 correct/decoy/refusal snapshots, plus sham and same-payload interventions; ≤0.5 GPU-h. **Kill:** harmless cache surgery moves logits as much as factual interventions, or effects fail to predict exact-answer errors. **Spec:** preserves the live-snapshot rule; diagnostic only initially. **Art:** P5, extending the house S3/S4 arbiter.

### 6. Translate the address book before translating the library — U3 × 65% = 1.95

Share cross-model routing addresses while retaining separate native K/V payloads for each reader. On migration, reharvest selected source records into the target repository off the serving path, so address portability can succeed even when direct payload translation fails.

**If it works:** upgrades preserve discoverability without asserting dialect equivalence. **Falsifier:** reuse the translation PoC's held-out identifiers, compare native and translated-index recall with shuffled and amnesia controls; CPU analysis then ≤0.5 GPU-h. **Kill:** key transfer cannot beat lexical-only routing, or required reharvest approaches a full-library rebuild. **Spec:** preserved; text is used only for offline deposit. **Art:** P1/P8; already proposed as a consolation path in the house translation primer.

### 7. Give corrections an explicit anti-memory — U3 × 60% = 1.80

Represent a correction as a small native tombstone graft saying which earlier binding is invalid and which version supersedes it. Deliberately test seating that negative witness with the current fact when ordinary L2 resolution cannot identify the relevant relation.

**If it works:** retractions and “no longer true” survive paraphrase and partial matches. **Falsifier:** 20 correction chains against current-only L2 mounts; ≤0.5 GPU-h. **Kill:** the old value reappears more often or consumes seats without benefit. **Spec challenge:** primer §4 article 1 discourages co-mount/readout adjudication; this proposes one tightly bounded exception because negation is itself content. **Art:** P2/P4; not a replacement for L2 by default.

### 8. Let a tiny attention expert carry the episode — U5 × 30% = 1.50

Fit 8–16 learned K/V slots to an episode while freezing the reader, then mount them as a third operator between exact grafts and FFN experts. Test held-out relations rather than matching hidden-output MSE alone.

**If it works:** some episodic state compresses into attention without the failed FFN bottleneck. **Falsifier:** two synthetic documents, withheld relational questions, native/amnesia/shuffled-slot controls; ≤1 GPU-h on a small model. **Kill:** only training questions transfer, corrections revive stale values, or native slots of equal size win. **Spec challenge:** §1's captured native K/V requirement; synthetic slots are explicitly a different proposal. **Art:** P6/P9; the house primer already names this third operator.

### 9. Vaccinate GRAPA against convincing wrong memories — U5 × 30% = 1.50

Train a small native reader on paired episodes where equally plausible mounts alternately contain the answer, a contradiction or no answer. Reward using the correct binding and abstaining on absence, with identifiers and task templates disjoint across train and evaluation.

**If it works:** the model learns a retrieval contract rather than a verbal uncertainty style. **Falsifier:** ≤1 GPU-h pilot on an available small checkpoint, then a frozen fresh-entity and unseen-decoy set. **Kill:** improvement vanishes with new templates or a trivial generator cue predicts answerability. **Spec challenge:** frozen-reader premise in §1; this belongs to the separate GRAPA program. **Art:** P1/P10; extend the board's existing abstention curriculum, not its stale “training live” status.

### 10. Train two readers to share a memory ABI — U5 × 25% = 1.25

Give two small native models a jointly trained K/V export–import interface while allowing their internal residual bases to differ. Make cross-reader episodic recall, rather than representation cosine, the interface's acceptance test.

**If it works:** a repository can outlive one model without fitting a translator for every replacement. **Falsifier:** a ≤1 GPU-h toy pair with matched data, private-interface controls and unseen episodic bindings; the full program would cost much more. **Kill:** identity works but cross-reader binding fails, or the shared interface materially damages either reader. **Spec challenge:** §1's frozen-model deployment assumption, only for a future GRAPA design. **Art:** P8/P9; no prior art known to me for this exact GRM ABI.

### 11. Make NVMe part of attention itself — U5 × 20% = 1.00

Stream a broad, low-precision repository score pass through bounded buffers and fetch exact native pages only for APA-style refinement. Let the external memory contribute to attention normalization without first admitting every contributor as a conventional arena mount.

**If it works:** retrieval selection and attention become one operation over far more memory than fits locally. **Falsifier:** ≤1 GPU-h replay of one real layer at 1K/10K pages, measuring end-to-end I/O plus output error against exact attention. **Kill:** PCIe/NVMe or quantization costs exceed route-and-mount, or position/dialect semantics cannot match the reference. **Spec challenge:** §1's fixed mounted arena and §4 article 2's individually mountable-node law. **Art:** P1/P7; APA refinement keeps approximate contributions, unlike BLASST's omission.

### 12. Compress the differences between versions, losslessly — U3 × 30% = 0.90

Store a corrected graft as an exact byte delta against its predecessor, with checksummed reconstruction before normal mounting. Keep explicit lineage and native payload fidelity while asking whether small textual edits create exploitable redundancy across K/V versions.

**If it works:** corrections become cheaper on NVMe without the quantization sweep's un-supersession failure. **Falsifier:** CPU-only compression analysis on a bounded, separately authorized payload sample; measure complete reconstructed bytes and decode latency. **Kill:** contextualization makes deltas high entropy, compression saves less than 20%, or cold-fetch latency rises more than 10%. **Spec:** preserved; this scout read no tensors. **Art:** P3; exact delta coding is established, its payoff here is unknown.
